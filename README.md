# Brooklyn Property Price Predictors

A data analysis project examining how factors associated with property sale prices in Brooklyn changed between 2003 and 2017, a period of significant gentrification.

## Research Question

How did the factors associated with property prices in Brooklyn change from the early 2000s to the late 2010s? In particular, did different property characteristics become associated with higher sale prices as neighborhoods and building types changed over time?

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Brooklyn property sales across three years—2003, 2010, and 2017—to examine how the relationship between property sale price and other property characteristics changed over time.

We investigated factors including neighborhood, building type, gross square footage, and building age to compare patterns at different stages of Brooklyn's gentrification.

## Key Findings

The neighborhoods and building types associated with higher average sale prices changed between 2003 and 2017.

The association between sale price and factors such as gross square footage remained relatively consistent over the period.

The association between sale price and building age also remained mostly consistent.

Overall, the results suggest that changes in neighborhood and building-type preferences were more pronounced than changes in the relationships between sale price and certain property characteristics.
